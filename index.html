About

DESIGN, TO ME, IS BEING CREATIVE WITHIN LIMITATIONS — THAT'S WHAT I LIKE MOST ABOUT MECHANICAL ENGINEERING.

I'm a second-year Mechanical Engineering student at UofT with a habit of taking things apart to see how they work — then trying to build something better. Most of my time goes into hands-on prototyping: CAD one day, a soldering iron or a bandsaw the next.

I currently co-lead the UTRA Combat Robotics team's weapon subsystem design, and I've spent the last two years moving through UofT's Praxis design studios, a matboard bridge that had to survive a 400N load, and a robotic camera arm — always chasing the same thing: a solution that's clever and actually manufacturable.

Outside the shop, I mentor younger students in CAD and robotics with my FIRST Robotics team, teaching the fundamentals I once had to learn myself.
